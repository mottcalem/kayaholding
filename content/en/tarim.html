Agriculture Sector

A vision of natural production, strong investment, and a sustainable future.

Strength from the Soil

Natural Production, Strong Investment, Sustainable Future

Kaya Holding expands its activities through strategic, sustainable, and value-added investments by bringing the strong experience and corporate expertise it has gained in the tourism and construction sectors into agriculture.

Within the Holding, production is carried out on a total of 2,150 dönüm of farmland—650 dönüm in Edirne, 1,000 dönüm in Uzunköprü, and 500 dönüm in Tekirdağ Çorlu. In line with the region’s climate and soil structure, staple crops primarily including wheat, canola, and sunflower are cultivated; the products obtained are contributed to the national economy through public procurement channels.

Diversified Production

Investments Expanding from Thrace to Antalya

Kaya Holding’s agricultural investments are diversified not only by large-scale field production in the Thrace region but also by fruit growing activities in Antalya. On an 82-dönüm agricultural area in Antalya, production is carried out with 580 pomegranate trees, while the development of 2,650 avocado saplings continues.

A portion of the pomegranate production is used to meet the needs of the Holding’s distinguished tourism facilities in line with high quality standards; the remainder is offered to the commercial market. Thus, quality, reliability, and traceability are essential throughout the process from production to consumption.

The newly planted avocado saplings form an important part of Kaya Holding’s long-term growth approach in agriculture. This investment, still in its development phase, is expected to increase product diversity in the coming periods and make a significant contribution to high value-added agricultural production.

Long-Term Vision

A Reliable Agricultural Production Model

Efficiency, quality, conservation of natural resources, and sustainability are adopted as core priorities in production processes. Combining modern agricultural practices with a corporate management approach, Kaya Holding builds a reliable agricultural production model through its land holdings in different regions, diversified product structure, and strong operational capability.

Kaya Holding regards its presence in the agriculture sector not merely as a new area of activity, but as a long-term investment that contributes to food supply security, regional development, employment, and the national economy.

Bringing together the value of the soil with the right investments, contemporary production methods, and a strong vision for the future, Kaya Holding continues its growth in agriculture with determination.
